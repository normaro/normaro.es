Privacy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This website

  • No cookies, no forms, no analytics, no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data of each request, such as your IP address, under its own privacy statement.
  • If you write to support@normaro.es, we use your address and your message only to answer you, and we do not pass them on to anyone. The mail is delivered through Forward Email to the mailbox we answer from (Gmail, Google). We keep it only as long as the conversation needs.

The Verifactu extension for Magento 2

  • The extension sends no data to Normaro. It has no telemetry and makes no requests to normaro.es.
  • To issue invoices, the shop sends the invoice data (customer name, company, NIF or VAT number, billing address, email, order lines and amounts, payment method, order and invoice numbers) to the shop's own Quaderno account. This happens under the shop's contract with Quaderno, and Quaderno processes the data under its privacy policy. Normaro has no access to that account or that data.
  • The Quaderno invoice number, status and links are stored in the shop's own Magento database.
  • The debug log (var/log/verifactu.log, only when the shop turns debug logging on) stays on the shop's server. The API key is never written to it, and NIF and VAT numbers are masked.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request to access or delete your messages: support@normaro.es. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority, AEPD.
